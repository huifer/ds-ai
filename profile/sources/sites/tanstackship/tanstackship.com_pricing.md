## Pricing FAQ

### What is Launch?

Launch is the lifetime entry point for independent developers and side projects. It includes access to all current templates, lifetime updates, a commercial license, and the architecture base for shipping quickly.

### Why is Pro the recommended package?

Pro includes everything in Launch and adds the systems that save the most time when building a SaaS: billing, auth, SEO, i18n, and priority support.

### Who is Team for?

Team is built for small teams, studios, and client work. You get everything in Pro plus a team license, onboarding support, and a private support channel.

### Are the licenses commercial-use friendly?

Yes. Every lifetime package includes a commercial license for your own products and client work.

### Do I get future releases?

Yes. All packages include updates for current systems and access to future template and stack releases.
