# Refund Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

The short version:

* We offer a 14-day, no-questions-asked refund on all premium templates
* Email us your order number within 14 days of purchase
* Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-10 business days
* Free templates have no charges and need no refund process

## 1. Eligibility

Refund requests are accepted for premium template purchases within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date. To be eligible, your refund request must be submitted before the 14-day window closes.

There is no requirement to explain your reason for the refund, though feedback is welcome and helps us improve our templates.

## 2. How to Request a Refund

Email[support@tanstackship.com](mailto:support@tanstackship.com) with:

* The email address associated with your account
* Your order number (found in your purchase confirmation email)
* The name of the template you purchased

We process refund requests within 1 business day. The refund itself will appear on your statement within 5-10 business days depending on your payment provider.

## 3. Exceptions

Refunds are not available in the following circumstances:

* More than 14 days have passed since the purchase date
* The template has been used to launch a commercial product with paying customers (in this case, you have received commercial value)
* The request is for a free template (no charge was made)
* Evidence of chargeback abuse (multiple refund requests across different accounts)

If you have an exceptional circumstance not covered above, contact us and we will review it on a case-by-case basis. We want you to be happy with your purchase.

## 4. Partial Refunds

We do not offer partial refunds. If you are eligible for a refund, you will receive the full purchase amount.

## 5. Contact

Questions about a refund? Contact our support team at[support@tanstackship.com](mailto:support@tanstackship.com).We typically respond within 1 business day.
