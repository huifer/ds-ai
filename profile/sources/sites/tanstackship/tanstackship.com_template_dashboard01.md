## FAQ

### Is Dashboard 01 a single-page template?

Yes. The initial detail implementation is designed around a single-page dashboard experience with multiple sections surfaced inside one workflow-oriented layout.

### Can I reuse it for internal tooling?

Yes. The structure is suited to internal tools, admin panels, and operational dashboards where teams need approval queues, summaries, and reporting views.

### Can I customize the cards and chart areas?

Yes. The template is intended as a starting point, so you can swap metrics, actions, tables, and visual modules to match your own product requirements.
